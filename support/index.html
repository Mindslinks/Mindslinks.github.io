Frequently asked questions

Most issues can be solved in a minute or two.

How do I move the catcher?

Touch the catcher near the bottom of the screen and drag left or right. Keep your finger down while you follow a falling item into its lane.

How do levels and points work?

Complete the catch goal to advance. Requested foods fill the current order and award a large bonus. Eight correct order catches activate five seconds of x4 Fever scoring. The point target helps determine your final level rating.

What happens every three levels?

Each three-level world builds toward a finale. The next world introduces a fresh background, a brief pacing reset, and gradually harder goals.

What do rotten food and rare fruits do?

Catching clearly marked rotten food breaks your combo and costs one heart. Passion fruit completes any active order. The exceptionally rare dragon fruit grants a major point bonus in later levels.

Where is my progress stored?

Your level, current run checkpoint, hearts, and best score are stored on your device without an expiration date. App updates do not erase this progress. Deleting the app removes local progress unless it is recovered through a device backup.

The game is not responding. What should I try?

Pause and resume the game first. If that does not help, close Grab & Grow completely and reopen it. Make sure you are running the newest App Store or TestFlight build.

Which devices are supported?

Grab & Grow supports iPhone running iOS 16.0 or later.
