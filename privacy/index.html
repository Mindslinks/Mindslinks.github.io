The short version

Grab & Grow: Food Catch is developed by Abdulkarim Tob. It does not require an in-game account and includes no advertising, analytics, social login or cross-app tracking software. Gameplay progress is saved on your device and, when available, in your private iCloud database. This policy also explains optional notifications, support email and this website.

Gameplay information

The game saves level progress, scores, your coin wallet and coin-based Booster purchases, Booster inventory and use, reward history, campaign completion, current-run checkpoints and remaining hearts. These records are used to operate the game and save or restore your progress.

Private iCloud progress

When you are signed in to iCloud and CloudKit is available, lasting campaign progress—including unlocked levels, scores, coins, Boosters and reward records—is synchronized through your private iCloud database. Current-run checkpoints and remaining hearts remain on the device. The developer does not operate a gameplay server and does not receive or view these private gameplay records. The game does not receive your name, email address or contact information through this synchronization.

Optional notifications and update checks

If you allow notifications, the game can schedule a local play reminder. If you enable game news, Apple’s CloudKit and Apple Push Notification service deliver published game announcements through a subscription associated with your iCloud account. You can turn these options off in the game or your device settings. The game also contacts Apple’s App Store lookup service to check whether a newer compatible public version is available. These features do not add advertising or analytics tracking.

Your choices and deletion

You can play without a separate game account. Local gameplay remains available without iCloud, subject to any already-confirmed required App Store update. Removing the app deletes its local gameplay data; permanent progress may remain in iCloud and be restored after reinstalling. In game settings, Restart Levels keeps balances and records while restarting level progression. Erase Everything removes saved progress and balances and synchronizes that reset when iCloud is available. These actions cannot be undone. You can also manage or delete the game’s stored iCloud data through your device’s iCloud storage settings.

Support email

If you email support, we receive the email address, message and attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, investigate the reported issue, protect the support channel from misuse and meet applicable legal obligations. Support correspondence is not linked to your private iCloud gameplay records or used for advertising. Messages are retained only as long as reasonably needed for those purposes. You may email us to request deletion of a support conversation, subject to any legal retention requirement.

Apple services

Apple provides iCloud, CloudKit, push notification delivery and App Store services and may process downloads and device diagnostics under its own privacy policy. The current game does not offer in-app purchases. The game contains no third-party advertising, analytics, social-login or tracking SDKs.

Children’s privacy

The game does not ask children or other players for names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, photos or voice recordings. Gameplay information is handled as described above. A parent or guardian may contact support about a privacy question.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not run advertising, analytics scripts or tracking pixels, and it has no account system or contact form. Your browser sends ordinary connection information, such as your IP address and browser information, to GitHub when requesting pages and images. GitHub handles hosting information under its own privacy statement. If you follow an App Store or email link, the destination service’s practices apply.
