Privacy Policy

Effective July 30, 2026

Overview

Grab & Grow does not require an in-game account and does not include advertising, analytics, social login, or cross-app tracking. The game stores gameplay progress on your device. When iCloud is available, permanent campaign progress is also stored in your private iCloud database for recovery and synchronization across devices.

Gameplay data

On your device, Grab & Grow stores your level progress, per-level stars, completed honey rewards, campaign completion, best score, booster inventory and use, win streak results, current-run checkpoint, and remaining hearts. This data is used only to save and restore gameplay.

iCloud synchronization

When the device is signed in to iCloud and CloudKit is available, the game synchronizes your unlocked level, per-level stars, completed honey rewards, campaign completion, best score, booster inventory and use, and win streak results through your private iCloud database. This lets lasting progress recover after reinstalling and remain available across your iPhone and iPad devices. Current-run checkpoints and remaining hearts stay on the device and are not synchronized.

The developer does not operate a gameplay server and does not receive or view this private gameplay data. The game does not receive your name, email address, or other contact information through iCloud synchronization.

Retention and control

Removing the app deletes its local gameplay data, but synchronized permanent progress may remain in iCloud and can be restored after reinstalling the app or on another device. You can reset gameplay progress from the game's settings. When iCloud is available, the fresh reset state is synchronized across your devices. You can manage or delete the app's stored iCloud data through your device's iCloud storage settings.

Apple services and third-party software

The game uses Apple's CloudKit service only for private gameplay-progress synchronization. Apple may also process App Store downloads, purchases, and diagnostic information under Apple's own privacy policy. The game includes no third-party advertising, analytics, social-login, or tracking software.

Children's privacy

The game does not ask children or other players for names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, photos, voice recordings, or other contact information. Gameplay progress is handled as described above.

Policy changes

If the game's data practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes take effect. The effective date above will also be revised.

Contact

If you contact support by email, we receive the email address and message you choose to send. We use that information only to answer your request, investigate the issue you reported, protect the support channel from misuse, and meet any applicable legal obligations. Support messages are not connected to your private iCloud gameplay data and are not used for advertising or tracking. They are kept only as long as reasonably needed for those purposes. You may email us to request deletion of a support conversation, subject to any legal retention requirement.
