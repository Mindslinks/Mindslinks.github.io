Privacy Policy

Effective July 11, 2026

Overview

Grab & Grow does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The game does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, or cross-app tracking.

Information stored on your device

Grab & Grow stores your level progress, current run checkpoint, remaining hearts, and best score locally on your device. This information is used only to continue your progress and is not sent to us. It does not expire. Removing the app removes this local data unless it is recovered through a device backup.

Third-party services

The game does not include third-party advertising, analytics, social login, or tracking software. Apple may process App Store downloads, purchases, and diagnostic information under Apple's own privacy policy.

Children's privacy

Grab & Grow does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other players.

Policy changes

If the game's data practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes take effect. The effective date above will also be revised.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent to our support team.
