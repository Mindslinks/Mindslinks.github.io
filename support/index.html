How do I catch food and slice giant fruit?

Move your catcher left and right under the falling food. Avoid rotten items and other hazards. When giant fruit appears, swipe through the fruit, then move the catcher underneath the pieces to collect them.

How do I activate Fever?

Build the Fever meter with healthy catches. When Fever starts, catches earn ×4 score for a short burst. The game’s HUD shows your current catch goal and Fever progress.

Where is my progress saved?

Your game saves progress on your device. When you are signed in to iCloud and CloudKit is available, lasting progress—including unlocked levels, scores, your coin wallet, reward records and Boosters—syncs through your private iCloud database. Current-run checkpoints and remaining hearts stay on the device.

My progress is missing or hasn’t synced. What should I do?

Check that your devices use the same Apple Account, that iCloud is available, and that you have an internet connection. Reopen the game and allow a little time for synchronization. Do not erase progress or delete iCloud data while troubleshooting. If progress still appears incorrect, email support with your device models, app versions and what happened.

What is the difference between Restart Levels and Erase Everything?

In the game’s settings, Restart Levels returns the campaign to Level 1 while keeping your coins, unused Boosters, best scores and reward history. Erase Everything removes saved progress and balances. These actions affect synced devices when iCloud is available and cannot be undone. Read the confirmation carefully before choosing either option.

The game has frozen or has no sound. What should I try?

Pause and resume if possible. Check the music and sound controls in the game’s settings and your device volume. If the issue continues, close and reopen the game, then check for an App Store update. Avoid uninstalling while investigating missing progress.

Can I play without an account or internet connection?

No separate in-game account is required. Local gameplay is available offline. iCloud synchronization, game news and update checks need a connection. If the game has already confirmed that a newer compatible public App Store version is required, you must update before continuing to play. Your saved progress is preserved.

How do I turn off notifications?

Play reminders and game news are optional. Use the game’s notification settings to change them, or manage all notifications for Grab & Grow in your device’s Settings. Game news requires iCloud availability.

Which devices and languages are supported?

Grab & Grow supports iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 16.0 or later. The game includes English and Arabic interfaces and supports Reduce Motion.

Are there in-app purchases?

The current version does not offer in-app purchases. Coins earned through play can be spent on in-game Boosters.
