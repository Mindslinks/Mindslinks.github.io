Frequently asked questions

Most issues can be solved in a minute or two.

How do I move the catcher?

Touch near the bottom of the screen and drag left or right to move the parcel under falling food. In cutting encounters, swipe across the giant food, then catch the pieces.

How do levels, hearts, and scores work?

Fill the green catch goal to complete the level. You do not need to reach a coin or score target to advance. Missing normal food costs one heart. Eight healthy catches fill the Fever meter and activate ×4 Score; replay levels to improve your Best Score.

What changes between chapters?

Explore 90 levels across nine chapters, with changing food patterns, hazards, and scenery. Town Trail brings rainy challenges, The Giant Garden features cutting encounters, and Chocolate Slice events ask you to cut a bar and catch its pieces.

How do Bear Shield and Fever Start work?

Bear Shield blocks your first missed grocery or rotten-item penalty and is used only when it protects you. Fever Start begins with the Fever meter at 4/8, so four healthy catches activate ×4 Score.

What should I avoid catching?

Avoid clearly marked rotten food and watch the warnings for incoming hazards. Catch healthy food in the parcel to fill your goal, and use Bear Shield for protection against one missed-grocery or rotten-item penalty.

Where is my progress stored?

Lasting progress—including unlocked levels, best records, mastery badges, claimed rewards, coin balance, booster inventory, and campaign completion—syncs through your private iCloud database when available. Your current-run checkpoint and remaining hearts stay on your device. If the app closes during a level, that level restarts when you return. Without iCloud, lasting progress remains available locally.

How do I reset or remove my progress?

Restart Levels returns the map to Level 1 while keeping the scores, rewards, and items listed in the confirmation. Claimed gifts cannot be collected again. Erase Everything removes all saved progress, scores, rewards, and items after a separate confirmation. Either choice also updates synced devices when iCloud is available.

The game is not responding. What should I try?

Pause and resume the game first. If that does not help, close Grab & Grow completely and reopen it. Make sure you are running the newest App Store or TestFlight build.

Which devices are supported?

Grab & Grow supports iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 16.0 or later.
